The terminal block: B on glass

B's structure (the command on its own, a status, the output, a footer with the line count and Copy) on D's dark glass, with the long output cut and "Show N more lines". Three ways of laying it out below, each with the same three calls. The sliders change the glass; read the values off them and I use them as they are. Hover and click everything.
In the app: the glass would be SwiftUI's glassEffect tinted dark on a rounded rectangle, or a material plus the same gradient and hairline if a long list of them is heavy to draw (every opened call is one). The cut at N lines and the line count are from what the engine sent: it keeps the first and last lines of the output, so "14 lines" is that, not the whole log.
