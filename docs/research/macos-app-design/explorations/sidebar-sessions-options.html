The sidebar: Home, Sessions by workspace, Carrier apart, Archive

What the sidebar has to do, from the review: Home is the Overview and also where a session starts (a message box, plus the state of everything running); Sessions are the conversations you start and are grouped by workspace (the folder you work in); Carrier is the automation (epics) and must read as a different thing; and archived sessions must be reachable. Five ways to do it, no rail. Switch what is selected, how much there is, and whether archived sessions are shown. All data is invented.
