Roles and identities

A role is a place in an epic and the skills it runs there: Author, the reviewers in parallel (up to four) and the Lead reviewer. An identity is the GitHub account that signs (a bot of Agent Forge or your own login). A role also needs a model (an account of Providers, a model, an effort). The options differ in where the model lives.
