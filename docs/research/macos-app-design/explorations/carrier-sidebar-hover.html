The hover card

The card of each state, with what it can carry as switches. The row says one thing; the card is for the person who stopped to look, so it answers "why, how far, who, what now". Turn sections off to see what the card is without them.
When it opensAfter the pointer rests on the row for about 0.6 s, so crossing the list opens nothing; once one is open, the next opens at once. It closes when the pointer leaves, on Escape and on scroll.
Keyboard and VoiceOverThe card is the row's tooltip, not a new control: ⌥-hover is not needed. VoiceOver already reads the row (title, state, reason); the card adds nothing a person cannot reach in the epic's own view.
If it has buttonsThe pointer has to travel from the row to the card (12 pt of air) and keep it open, which is a bridge to build and test. Without buttons the card is only information and the actions stay in the row's context menu.
What it is made ofThe snapshot has it all except two things: the age as its own field and the PR's checks. The "why" sentence is the app's own text for each kind of stop (needsHumanKind), because needsHumanReason is a short code.
