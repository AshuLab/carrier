Epic view, option A — who is in each step (round 2)

The header of a session says "Claude · Builder · Ship": the AI tool, but not who acts. Two things identify a step. On GitHub it is the profile (an Agent Forge bot or your own login: the name on the PR, the comments, the commits). On the AI side it is the account (the login of the CLI, e.g. pro), the model and the effort. The engine's session carries harness, model, account (provider) and role; the profile comes from agents.author.profile / agents.reviewer.profile by role (the Fixer is the author).

Invented names for the demo: bots ashu-author[bot] and ashu-reviewer[bot], accounts pro and max. Say what the real ones look like.

A1 · The identity is the name; the tool and account go on the second line

The bot's picture (the one GitHub shows) carries the harness' mark on its corner. First line: the identity, then what it does. Second line, quiet: the account, model and effort. Whoever you see on the PR is whoever you see here.
