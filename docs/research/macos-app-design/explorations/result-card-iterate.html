The agent's closing card, without the summary

Decision. The summary stays in the result (the next reviewer reads it) but the app does not draw it: it is the engine's hand-off between agents, and the person already has the agent's own last message. What is left for the card is what the engine knows: the outcome, the CI verdict with the evidence behind it, the reason of a stop, how long it took, and (in the JSON already, not yet sent to the app) the blockers, the resolved ones and the observations. Four ways to lay that out. Click a row to open it. The dashed items need the engine's sessions reply to carry the lists.
Rules in every option. A stop's reason is never behind a click: it is why a person is needed. The CI evidence is the agent's claim (Carrier does not run anything), so it sits one click away, in the same flat block as a command's output. A blocker row links to its thread. The time is the session's own (finishedAt − startedAt), not part of the result.
