Menu bar: what opens when you click the icon

Today it is a native menu: a grey status line, the four controls (Start is greyed out while running), then the epics under a tiny "Epics" header, then the window items. Problems I see in the screenshot: the status line is the least readable thing in the menu; the epics (what you open the menu for) sit below the controls; a disabled "Start Carrier" while running is noise; "Pause / Check / Stop" have the same weight as "Open window"; no hint of what needs you. Hover the rows to see the highlight.
What changes in the app per option. A is pure NSMenu work (reorder, hide irrelevant items, SF Symbols, shortcuts): about an hour, zero risk. B keeps the native menu but adds two custom-view items (header and button row): custom views in a menu do not highlight or close the menu on their own, so each needs a handler. C is a different surface (NSPopover or a borderless glass panel) so all the menu behaviours are ours (esc, click outside, keyboard): the biggest win visually, the biggest job. D is B with counters in place of the long status text.
