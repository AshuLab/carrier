One button in the box: provider · model · reasoning · access

The four chips under the box become one pill at the bottom left of the box (the send button stays bottom right). The pill shows the provider's icon, the model and the reasoning level; the panel it opens holds the four choices. Click around: the choices are live and the pill follows them. Four ways to cut the panel.

A · One list, everything visible

The four choices stacked in one panel: provider as a switch, models as a list, reasoning as a segment, access as a list. Nothing hidden, no second click; it is the tallest panel.
