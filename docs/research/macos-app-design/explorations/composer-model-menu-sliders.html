One button: provider tabs, models, and two sliders

The pill at the bottom left of the box opens one panel. Top: the providers as icon tabs. Under them, that provider's models. Bottom: a slider for reasoning and another for access. Everything is live: pick a provider, search, drag a slider (or click a dot), and the pill follows.

How it reads. The provider changes the list and the access steps (each provider lists its own). The reasoning slider has the steps the chosen model supports; a model with none (Haiku here) greys it with the reason. The access slider goes from the most careful step to the freest, and its fill goes green, orange, red; the pill shows a flag when access is not the careful default.
