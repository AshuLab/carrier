Roles and Identities — navigable

The Carrier group of Settings with the split settled: Roles (today's Agents) holds everything a role runs with (identity, model, effort, persona, failover, skills), Identities is who signs on GitHub. Providers stays where it is, in the App group, because the chat host reads its accounts too; it is only linked from here. Everything is clickable: rows open screens, pills open menus, ‹ › go back and forward. Try: add a reviewer, turn the lead off and on, open an identity from a role, override a repo.

What writes whatA role's fields write agents.<role> (extra reviewers: agents.extraReviewers[n], their failover inline). Identities writes nothing in agents: it reads which roles sign as each and adds a profiles entry when a role picks a new bot.
A new reviewerLike the lead today: it is a draft in the page until it has an identity and a model, then the whole entry is sent at once, so the file never holds half a reviewer. Removing one renumbers the ones after it; running epics keep what they started with.
ReposThe Reviewer and the lead are overridden field by field, as the Author. The more reviewers are one list that replaces the global one whole (Same as everywhere, Own list, None), because that is how the engine reads it.
