Epic view, option A1 — the centered session row (round 3)

The identity row of a session sits in the middle of the feed, with no background and no shell, like a separator in a chat: it says who comes next. The whole row is the control: a click folds and unfolds the session under it, and it carries the summary when folded. When sessions pile up, the earlier ones group into one row of stacked avatars. Click a row to fold it.
