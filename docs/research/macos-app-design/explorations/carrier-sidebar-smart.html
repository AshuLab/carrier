Carrier sidebar: one smart line and a card

The row is B. Its second line says the one thing that matters in each state; everything else is in a card that opens beside the sidebar when the pointer rests on a row (hover the right-hand list). Left: the list as approved, for comparison. On the right the repo is its icon alone: no GitHub mark and no name in the row.

The line, by state. Needs attention: why, in its colour. Running: what the agent is doing now. Up next: what it waits for. Ready: the PR. The number stays first in all of them. Round, blockers, slices, the agents, the branch and the times are in the card, not in the row. The repo's name is not in the row: its icon (initials and colour) says it, and the card and the tooltip spell it out. The card replaces today's text tooltip; it needs no new data beyond the age and the PR's checks (not in the snapshot yet).
