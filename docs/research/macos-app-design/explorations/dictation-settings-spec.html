Guide to build it

What to reuse, what is new, the tokens, the states and the accessibility. Sizes are SwiftUI's macOS text styles at the default text size (.title3 15, .body 13, .callout 12, .subheadline 11, .caption and .caption2 10), read from the code, not measured from a screenshot.

3. New

PieceWhat it is
SettingsGroup accessoryThe group's title row needs a trailing control for the recognizer filter. Add an optional accessory view to SettingsGroup; the default stays as it is.
SpeechModelRowA HoverRow laid out in fixed columns, 20 (radio) · flexible (name and recognizer · licence) · 64 (size) · 150 (languages) · 104 (text appears) · 158 (action), 10 apart, row padding 8, dividers in the card like the rest. Build it with HStack and fixed widths, not SwiftUI's Table: a Table inside a scrolling page brings its own scrolling and look, and the card style would be lost.
Column headerA row of .caption2 semibold secondary uppercase, the same style as a group's title.
StatusChipA capsule with .caption medium text, 8 across and 1 up, filled with its color at 16%: accent for Live, neutral (primary at 9%, secondary text) for When you stop, green for Allowed, red for Not allowed. The app has several private chips (EpicView, ReviewTabView, ReposTab, AttachmentChip) and no shared one; this is the moment to make one.
Download stateA model has one of: not downloaded, downloading (a thin accent bar, the percentage with .monospacedDigit(), a cancel button), failed (a red .caption reason and Retry), downloaded, in use. The state comes from the core; the row only draws it.

  • Color. Everything colored comes from the theme: Color.themeAccent, never a literal. Green and red are the system's and appear only for the microphone state and for errors.

6. States and behaviour (proposed; the spec confirms)

The page

  • Off. The switch is the one row that stays live in its card; the rows under it (Speech model, Language, Microphone) and the whole Speech models group are drawn at 40% and take no clicks. Storage and Reset stay live, so a person can free the disk with the feature off. The line under the switch says what off means. Off removes the microphone from the message box, asks macOS for nothing and never listens; what was downloaded stays on disk. Turning it off while a dictation is on stops it and keeps the text.
  • First use. Apple's model in use, nothing downloaded, Language on the language of macOS (Apple takes one language, so Automatic is not offered).
  • Choosing. The popup lists downloaded models; a click on a downloaded row does the same. Choosing a model changes what Language offers, and a language it does not take falls back to the first it offers.
  • Deleting. Only a downloaded model that is not in use has a trash; Apple's has none, it is the system's. To free the one in use, switch first.
  • Storage. The size of the downloaded models, in the Carrier folder, and Show in Finder.

Errors

  • Download failed. The reason in red in the action column, and Retry. The reason is the cause the core knows (no connection, no space on the disk, the file did not check), not a generic message.
  • Microphone denied. A red chip and Open System Settings, which opens the Microphone pane; the line says what it stops.
  • Cancelled download. The row returns to Download with no trace left on disk.
  • Quit mid-download. The partial file is deleted at the next start; the row shows Download.

7. Accessibility (proposed)

  • A model row is one button: its label reads the name, recognizer, size, languages and how the text appears, and it carries the selected trait when it is in use. The trash and Retry are their own buttons with labels ("Delete Whisper Tiny").
  • The progress is a value, "Downloading Parakeet TDT v3, 40 percent", updated at most each 10 percent so VoiceOver does not talk over the person; the finish is announced.
  • Group titles and the page title carry the header trait, as the other pages do. Color never carries a state alone: the chips and the radio have words and shapes too.
  • Every control works from the keyboard; the filter is a segmented picker, and the rows are reachable in order.
