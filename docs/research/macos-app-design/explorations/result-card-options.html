The agent's closing card

What it is. When an agent finishes it writes a small result file ($CARRIER_RESULT_PATH): an outcome (completed, changes requested, needs a human), a summary in its own words and, for a completed one, a verification: the CI verdict and the evidence it checked. Carrier shows that under the session: the green Completed pill, the summary, and the small grey line, which is that verification (CI passed: <evidence>). It is the agent's own claim, not something Carrier checked. Problems: the summary repeats what the last message said right above, the grey line is long and tiny, and nothing says "this is the result" apart from the pill. Four ways to give it shape; switch the outcome to see the other cases.
What the data allows. Four fields: outcome, summary (Markdown text), verification.ci (a word: passed, failed, …) and verification.evidence (free text, long), plus reason for needs_human. No links as data: PR and CI URLs are only inside the text. The summary can repeat the last message because agents are told to write both; a real fix is on the prompt side (a shorter summary), not in the app.
