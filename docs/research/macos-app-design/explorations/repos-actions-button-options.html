Repos: the actions button

Today it is a bare “⋯” with no outline, small and easy to miss, and it is the only way to open the repo, reveal it or stop watching it. Five ways of showing it, each with the open menu, now with icons. Click a button to open its menu; hover the rows. “Reveal clone in Finder” is greyed, with the reason, on the second row, which has no clone yet.
  • Why “Reveal clone in Finder” was grey: it is only on when the folder exists. Carrier clones a repo when it first needs it, so a repo that never ran an epic (agent-forge and universal-logger here) has none yet. The menu now says so.
  • What I would pick: A, a round glass button with the dots, always visible: it matches the app's other glass buttons and nobody has to discover it. B is the quietest and closest to Finder and Mail, but hides the only way in until you hover. E adds a shortcut for the most common action, at the price of two controls per row.
