# Settings

Settings in its own window, its sidebar, its search and where each group of pages is described.

Settings is a window of its own (`Carrier/SettingsWindow.swift`: an `NSWindow` hosting a `NavigationSplitView`, as the main window is), not a sheet: it does not stop the main window being used, it keeps its place, size and last page (`CarrierSettingsWindow` autosave, `SettingsNavigator`), and ⌘W closes it.
The sidebar lists eighteen pages in four groups, each page a colored tile with its symbol (`SettingsPage`, `SettingsSidebar`): App (General, Providers, Appearance, Notifications, Keybindings, Dictation, Keep awake; no heading, and Providers is here because both the engine and the chat host read its accounts), Workspace (Chat, Sessions, Projects, Sidebar, Terminal), Carrier (Agents, Repos, Behavior) and System (Engine, Health, About, last).
The chosen page is on the right under the window's toolbar: the page's name as its title and its line as its subtitle, ‹ › to go back and forward through the pages visited (⌘[ and ⌘], `SettingsHistory`), the page's note (why Behavior cannot save, with Ask Carrier again) and, on a page that has a Reset, a ⋯ menu with Reset this page….
The menu's Settings…, ⌘, and the sidebar's Settings row open it on the page it was left on (`WindowController.showSettings`; it opens the main window too when it was closed, since the pages read what Carrier answers to it), or on the page a caller asks for (the Open Settings button of the stopped-Carrier view asks for Engine, and a reason that points to Health opens Health).

## Search

The field at the top of the sidebar (⌘F) finds a page, a group or a row by the words of its title, its group, its page or its description, all of them, in any order, ignoring case and accents (`settingsResults`); the results are listed in the sidebar under their pages, the words found marked, and choosing one opens its page, scrolls to the row and shows it for a moment.
What can be found is `settingsIndex` (`CarrierCore/SettingsIndex.swift`, `SettingsIndexTests`): a row finds its place from its page, its group and its title (`settingsAnchor`), so a new row is added to the index and nothing else.
`SettingsIndexDriftTests` reads the views as text and fails when a title a page draws is not in the index, or one in the index is no longer written in the sources (a title that is only a value, such as an account's, is listed in the test as not searched).

## Reset

A page with many controls gives its Reset to the window (`SettingsPageBody(reset:)`), and the ⋯ menu of the toolbar asks "Reset <page>?" before it runs (an alert: Cancel, or Reset in red).
The ⋯ menu is only there on a page that has a Reset: Providers, Agents, Repos, Health, Engine and About have nothing to reset and show no menu.
The Dictation page has its Reset on its first screen only ([dictation](dictation.md#the-dictation-page)); on its screens the ⋯ menu is not drawn.
Behavior and Sessions keep what a Reset did before (the engine's eight values back to `EngineConfig()`, the preferences and the two ages of `chat.json`), now from the same menu.

## Where something that needs fixing shows

What Carrier is missing (`carrier` or `node` not found, or being looked for) shows as a banner on the Engine and Health pages only, and as a badge on their rows in the sidebar; it no longer sits over every page.

## The pages

- App: [settings-app](settings-app.md) (General, Providers, Appearance, Notifications, Keybindings), [dictation](dictation.md#the-dictation-page) (Dictation, after Keybindings: it is global, not of the chat) and [keep-awake](keep-awake.md) (Keep awake, last of the group).
- Workspace: [Sessions](#sessions-page) below (Chat, Sessions, Sidebar), [Projects](#projects-page) and [Terminal](settings-app.md#terminal).
- Carrier: [settings-carrier](settings-carrier.md) (Agents, Repos, Behavior).
- System: [settings-carrier](settings-carrier.md) (Engine), [settings-health](settings-health.md) (Health) and About.
- About (`Carrier/AboutTab.swift`, last of the sidebar): the app's icon, name and version with the links of the About panel (`aboutLinks`: Release notes, Documentation, GitHub) and a row Star Carrier on GitHub with its button (it opens the repository, `repositoryURL`); Updates (Check now or the step of an update found, Check by itself, Install by itself and Update delay: None, 1, 3, 7, 14 or 30 days, `updates.delayDays`, `updateDelayChoices`); and the engine's own update with Try again.
The delay holds back, for that many days after its date in the appcast, a release that Sparkle finds on its own (`UpdateDelay`, the updater's delegate, ending that check as "no update" and not as a failure, so the pill shows nothing); Check now and a critical update ignore it, and a release with no date is offered.

## Chat text

The Chat page's Conversation group has Message text size (11 to 20 pt, 13 by default), Line spacing (Tight, Normal, Loose) and Message spacing (Compact, Comfortable, Spacious: 8, 12 and 20 pt between messages), each apart from the app's Text size and Density (user defaults `chat.textSize`, `chat.lineSpacing` and `chat.messageSpacing`).
Text size and Density in Appearance no longer reach the messages' text or the gap between them; the rest of a conversation (tool rows, times, notices, the fields of a structured report) still follows the app's Text size, and the command pills of the bubble keep their fixed size.
The agent's answer (`MarkdownView`, its tables at 12.5/13 of the size, and its link hit areas, laid out again when a setting changes), the person's bubble and a subagent's brief read them through `.chatText()` (`ScaledFont.swift`); the two bubbles add no room between their lines at Normal, as before, and only what Loose has over it; the preview under the group follows each change, and Reset on the page restores them.

## Sessions page

Settings has a Sessions page (`Carrier/SessionsTab.swift`, `SettingsPage.sessions`, in the Chat group after Sidebar, "How sessions start and age out") with everything a person sets about how a session starts, ages and is deleted; the Chat page keeps only the message box and the conversation.
Its groups are New sessions ("Starts with", the provider, model, reasoning and access a new session opens with, "Work in", the base folder or a new worktree, and "Start from", where a new worktree is cut), Idle sessions, Archive and delete, With these ages and Reset.
"Start from" is the chat's `worktreeStart` in `chat.json`: Remote branch (latest), which is what the row shows when the file does not say, or Local branch.
A change is saved as it is made (`applyingWorktreeStart` in `CarrierCore/WorktreeStart.swift`, written the way the ages are: the file's other keys are kept, Remote is left out of the file, a file that is not a JSON object is never overwritten), and `worktreeStart(chatJSON:)` reads it back (`remote` when the key is missing or is not one of the two values).
The row is off, with the words "Only for a new worktree", while "Work in" is the base folder (`worktreeStartOffReason`), and on again when it is a new worktree; it stays in the search while it is off.
The group's footer says that Remote fetches the branch first and falls back to the local one when it cannot be reached.
Idle sessions has "Sleep after", the chat's `idleCloseMinutes` in `chat.json`: 10 minutes (what the row shows when the file does not say), 30 minutes or 1 hour (`IdleClose` in `CarrierCore/IdleClose.swift`).
It is how long a session nobody opens keeps its agent running before the host closes it; the session stays in the list as asleep and a message wakes it, and the one you have open is read all the time and stays.
A change is saved as it is made (`applyingIdleClose`, written the way the start is: the file's other keys are kept, 10 minutes is left out of the file, a file that is not a JSON object is never overwritten), and `idleClose(chatJSON:)` reads it back (10 minutes when the key is missing or is not one of the three values the host accepts); the host reads the file every few seconds, so nothing is restarted.
The row is in the search by what a person would type for it (sleep, idle, memory, asleep, close, agent, timeout, inactive, minutes, hour).
Archive and delete has the session clock's two ages, `archiveAfterDays` and `deleteAfterDays` of the chat's `<folder>/chat.json`: each a number of days or Never, shown with the values the file has (15 and 30 when it has none), and "Ask before deleting a session".
A change of an age is saved as it is made (`applyingSessionAges` in `CarrierCore/SessionAges.swift`, as `applyingProjectsFolder` does: the file's other keys are kept, an age equal to its default is left out, a file that is not a JSON object is never overwritten); the host reads the file each pass, so nothing is restarted.
Delete is off, with the reason, while archive is Never, and an age that breaks the host's rule (a number under 1, or delete not greater than archive) is not saved and the field says why (`sessionAgeProblems`, `deleteAgeOffReason`).
With these ages says in dates what the ages do to a session left alone from today (`sessionAgesLine` in `CarrierCore/SessionAges.swift`, given the date of the day: "Is archived on Nov 28 and deleted on Dec 13. Both ages count from its last activity", with the year only for a date that is not in this year; never archived or deleted with Archive at Never; archived and never deleted with Delete at Never; not shown while an age is refused), and, always, that a session that is working, waiting for you or open is looked at again later.
Reset (the toolbar's, with its question) puts back "Starts with", "Work in", "Start from" (to Remote, in `chat.json`), "Sleep after" (to 10 minutes, in `chat.json`), the delete question and the two ages to 15 and 30 days in `chat.json`; the Reset of Chat does not touch them.

## Projects page

Settings has a Projects page (`Carrier/ProjectsTab.swift`, `SettingsPage.projects`, in the Workspace group between Sessions and Sidebar, "The folders you work in"), the list of the projects a person works on ([the spec](../specs/projects.md), [0038](../adr/0038-a-project-is-an-entry-of-projects-json-identified-by-its-folder.md)).
It is a full-width list, found in the search by its name.
With no projects it shows an empty state with Add project; with some it shows a search field (matching name, remote and tag), a Group choice (None or Tag), a wrapping row of filter chips (All, each tag in use, Watched: only the ones Carrier watches, `ProjectFilter.watched`, Local only and Hidden: only the hidden ones, `ProjectFilter.hidden`), an Add project button and the list with Project, Tags, Last active, Disk and Carrier columns; Carrier says Watching or Not watched (`ProjectRow.carrierText`, `projectsWithWatched`: the project's `owner/name` is in the engine config's repos, compared without regard to case, read when the page opens).
Rows are ordered pinned first and then by name, or by when a session last worked in the project when the Last active title is clicked (most recent first, a second click reverses it, and a project no session worked in is last either way; clicking Project goes back to A to Z), or by what it takes on disk when the Disk title is clicked (biggest first, a second click reverses it, and a project never measured is last either way), the sorted title showing a small chevron for its direction (`ProjectOrder`, `ProjectSortKey`, `projectList(order:)`); the list can also be grouped under each project's first tag and "untagged" when grouped by Tag, with "Nothing matches" when the filters leave nothing; each row shows the project's tile (its chosen image, its chosen colour and mark, or initials on a colour made from its name when none is chosen), its name (the folder's name when none is set), the GitHub `owner/name` of its `origin` or else its path, its tags as chips, how long ago a session last worked in it or a dash when none did (`projectsWithLastActive`: the latest `updatedAt` of the sessions whose base folder is the project's path, compared with `~` expanded, symlinks resolved and no trailing slash, derived at each draw and never written to `projects.json`), what it takes on disk (`formatDiskSize`: "6.4 GB", "75 MB" or "310 KB"; the host's last figure for its folder and its worktrees together, `projectsWithDisk`) or a dash when the host has not measured it yet, a pin mark when pinned, and a chevron.
The host measures in the background and keeps each figure with when it was taken (`projectDisk`, behind the `projects` capability): when the page opens the list shows what the host has and asks again for the projects never measured or measured a day ago or more, which the host measures one at a time, and it reads the host again each second only for the folders being measured, so a figure shows when it lands (`ProjectsModel.refreshDisk`, `follow`).
A host that does not answer leaves the dashes.

A hidden project's row is drawn dimmed and says "Hidden" with an eye-off mark under its name (a folder that is not found says that instead).
The Projects page ends with a line saying where new clones go ("New clones go to …") and a Change… button that writes `projectsFolder` in `chat.json` using the same rule as the session sheet, refusing folders inside Carrier's own folders.
The remote is read from the repository each time (`originRepo(ofFolder:)` in `CarrierCore/ProjectsList.swift`, the `origin` of its `.git/config`) and is never stored; an `origin` that is not GitHub's, or no repository, shows the path.
Add project opens a sheet (`AddProjectSheet`, driven by `projectSheetState` in `CarrierCore/ProjectSheet.swift` in its Settings mode) with one search and path field, Suggested recent folders that are not yet projects, drag and drop from Finder, keyboard navigation (Return, up and down arrows, Escape, and ⌘O), and Open a folder… in its footer.
Typing filters the suggested folders, and a path starting with `~` or `/` offers Add folder (or Already a project · Open when that folder is already in `projects.json`); adding writes an entry with `origin` `added` and the folder's name (`ProjectsFile.add`: the file is read again just before, and every other entry and key is kept), closes the sheet, and opens the new project's page.
A folder inside Carrier's own folders (`engine`, `chat/state`, `chat/logs`, `chat/worktrees`, `chat/pending`, `chat/sessions`, with symlinks resolved), a file, a folder that cannot be read, or a worktree linked to another project is refused with the reason in orange (`projectFolderRefusal` in `CarrierCore/WorkspaceSources.swift`, `ProblemLine`), and writes nothing.
A folder whose worktree directory (`<owner>/<repo>`, or `local/<folder name>` without a GitHub origin, compared without regard to case) is the one of another project is refused the same way, with the other project named, whether it comes from the sheet, a drop or Locate… (`worktreeClashRefusal` in `CarrierCore/WorkspaceSources.swift`, `ProjectsModel.addRefusal`).
The host's `checkWorktreeClash` in `src/chat/names.ts` is the same rule for what it registers (a clone started from Settings that would clash is not registered and fails with the same words, the clone left as it is), and both tests read `macos/Tests/Fixtures/worktree-clash.json`.
A folder that already is a project, written any way (`~`, a trailing slash, a symlink), writes nothing and opens that project's page.
The same field takes a GitHub address or `owner/name` (`repoReference`) and searches the person's repositories (the host's `githubRepos`, five pages of thirty at most, with each one's description and last push and a Private chip): a pasted repository is the result "Clone owner/name" with "Clones into <projects folder>/<owner>/<name>" and Choose…, which changes the parent folder for that clone only and leaves `projectsFolder` as it is; a listed one that already is a project (its `origin`) shows Already a project · Open instead of Clone.
A pasted repository that is not among the person's own is looked up with `repoBranches` once typing pauses, and one the login cannot see shows "Your GitHub login can't see this repository. Check the name, or sign in with another account." in orange with Clone off (`repoNotVisibleLine`, told from a failure of `gh` by the host's words, `isRepoNotVisible`); a missing or signed-out `gh` shows the host's own wording for it.
A host whose hello lacks `projects` shows the clone results with Clone off and the words that the host is older and to restart it (`projectsNeedNewerHost`).
Clone asks the host for `cloneProject` and closes the sheet: the host clones in the background, the list shows the project as Cloning with git's latest progress line and without a chevron (it cannot be opened), and when git ends the host writes the entry with origin `cloned` (`added` for a folder that was already there and only fetched) and the row becomes a normal project; a failure stays in its row in orange with the host's reason and a Dismiss button (`dismissClone`).
`ProjectsModel` asks the host for its clones when the page opens and, while one is running, once a second (`projectClones`); a second clone of the same repository while one runs is the same clone.
Add project never makes Carrier watch what it clones; watching is the switch on the project's page.
The sheet is shared with New session, which opens it in its own mode (`ProjectSheetMode.newSession`, [New session](new-session.md#the-draft-and-the-workspace)): there a choice only fills the plan, the projects that are not hidden come first, a repository says it is cloned when the first message is sent and offers "Let Carrier watch this repo", and the sheet has no Choose… for a clone's parent.
The sheet's footer has New project…, which swaps the sheet for a form (`NewProjectForm` in `CarrierCore/ProjectSheet.swift`): a Name and a "Create in" line showing `<projects folder>/local/<name>` with Choose… for this one project only (`projectsFolder` stays as it is, and a folder inside Carrier's own is refused as it is for a clone).
A name that is not letters, digits, `_`, dots and dashes (starting with a letter, a digit or `_`, at most a hundred) is refused with the reason in orange (`projectNameProblem`, the host's own pattern), and so is a name whose folder is already there or that already is a project (`ProjectsModel.isTaken`); Create stays off while either is shown.
Create asks the host for `newProject`: it makes the folder, runs `git init -b main` in it and writes the entry with origin `created`, the sheet closes and the new project's page opens; the host refuses again what the app refused (a folder that is there, a name it does not take, a parent inside Carrier's own folders, a `projects.json` that does not match its schema) and then writes nothing.
Back returns to the chooser with what it had read.
Choosing a row opens the project as a screen of its own, a place of the window's history (‹ goes back to the list) in a centred column, with the project's name and its remote or path in the toolbar, and a Pin button in the page's header.
Its Identity group holds Name (emptied, it goes back to the folder's name, and a blank name is never written), Icon (Change… opens a popover of eight colours, eight existing `AppIcon` marks stored by the icon's name, and Choose image… to pick an image copied at most 256 points into `app/project-icons` as a PNG, cleared and deleted when a colour or mark is chosen afterwards, with a missing copy falling back to colour and mark) and Tags (Return adds a tag, trimmed, lower case and without repeats, and a cross removes it), each change written at once while keeping every other key.
Under Identity, a New sessions here group has the "Show when starting a session" switch (`ProjectsModel.setHidden`, which writes `hidden` and, when the switch is on again, takes the key out and changes nothing else); off, it says the project does not appear in the new session picker and that nothing is deleted.
The same group has Agent (Same as everywhere, then the configured agents), Work in (Default, Base folder, New worktree) and Base branch (Default branch, then the project's branches, remote ones by their bare name), which write `defaults.agent`, `defaults.workIn` (`baseFolder` or `worktree`) and `defaults.baseBranch` through `ProjectsModel.setDefault`; a value left on the default takes its key out (`applyingProjectDefaultsEdit`) and `defaults` goes with the last key, so nothing is written for it.
Work in and Base branch are shown only when the host lists branches for the folder, so a folder that is not a git repository has just Agent.
A new session in the project opens with these, over Settings > Sessions ([new session](new-session.md#a-projects-defaults)).
Hiding takes the project out of the sheet New session opens (among the projects and among the suggested folders) and nowhere else: it stays in this list, its sessions stay in the sidebar, and Settings' Add project still recognises it as Already a project.
Under it, a Worktrees and disk group (only for a project whose folder is there) lists the main clone with its branch ("Main clone" under it) and then each worktree Carrier made for sessions of the project, in the order git lists them.
A worktree's row says its branch, then the session that made it ("No session" when that session is gone, "Untitled session" when it has no title yet) and its age as the Time format says it, and ends in a chip with its state: Session open, Uncommitted changes, Merged (the session's pull request is merged), Not pushed (commits no other branch holds) or Clean, the first that applies.
A worktree with a detached HEAD says "Detached HEAD" for its branch.
The host answers it when the page opens (`projectWorktrees`, behind the `projects` capability): only worktrees git lists for the project's folder that lie under Carrier's worktrees folder and are still on disk, so one git still lists whose folder is gone is not shown.
A host without the capability is told to restart, and a folder git cannot read shows the host's reason in orange in the group.
The group starts with Total, which says what the project takes on disk (the main clone and the worktrees together, with a small spinner and "Measuring now" while the host measures it, else "Measured 3h ago", or "Not measured yet"), and a bar under it with one segment for each folder, the main clone first, as wide as its share of the total (`DiskBar`, `diskShares`).
Each row ends with a dot in its segment's colour and its size, a dash for a folder the host has not measured (a worktree made since).
Opening the page asks the host for a fresh figure of this project (`projectDisk` with `refresh: now`) and shows the last known one meanwhile, so a project that was measured shows its sizes at once and they change when the new figure lands.
A project whose folder is gone has no Worktrees and disk group, so no figure, and a dash in the list.
A project whose folder is there but is not a git repository (`isGitRepository`: it has no `.git`) is a project like any other in the list, which searches, filters and sorts it, and a session started in it works in its folder.
Its page has no Worktrees and disk group, no base branch row and no Carrier switch, and says why in one line under New sessions here ("Not a git repository, so it has no worktrees, base branch or Carrier switch."); its Work in choice offers only the base folder.
What a page has is a pure function of the row (`ProjectPage`, `ProjectsListTests`).
Each worktree's row has a Remove button before its size (the main clone has none) that asks the host for `removeProjectWorktree`: only the worktree's folder goes, with what git ignores in it, and its branch, its commits and its session stay, so the session's next message brings the folder back on its branch.
The button is disabled, with "Its session is open" as its tooltip, while the worktree's session is open; with uncommitted changes it is pressed and the host refuses, and its words ("It has uncommitted changes") replace the row's second line in orange until the next try.
A project that has a merged worktree also has a Clean up row, "Merged worktrees nothing blocks: 3 worktrees, 1.2 GB", with a Clean up button that asks "Clean up 3 worktrees, 1.2 GB?" and says the folders go and the branches and sessions stay.
What it takes is every worktree whose session's pull request is merged and that nothing blocks (`cleanProjectWorktrees`, which the page asks without `run` for the figures and with it to act); one that has uncommitted changes or an open session is skipped, and a "Last clean up" row says what went and, for each one skipped, its folder's name and the reason.
A worktree with no session, or whose session has no merged pull request, is never in Clean up, though it can be removed by hand.
After a removal or a clean-up the page asks for a fresh figure of the project, so the total and the bar follow the folders that went.
The size of each folder is what git does not count: the host walks the folder when Clean up asks, so the page asks only when a worktree is merged.
After Worktrees and disk, a Carrier group has one switch, "Let Carrier watch this project" (`ProjectWatchRow`, `projectWatchRow`, `saveWatch` in `CarrierCore/ProjectWatch.swift`), on when the project's `owner/name` is in the `repos` of `engine.json` as the file is when the page opens.
Turning it on adds the repository with the Repos page's writer (`applyingRepos`, through `saveConfig`: the file is read again just before, every other key and every other repo's fields are kept) and asks the engine to reload (`ReloadStatus`); turning it off asks "Stop watching owner/name?" with the Repos page's words about the project's epics (`removalText`) and then removes only that entry.
The row's second line is the Repos page's note (`settingsNote`: Carrier applies it to the next epic, or reads it when it starts when Carrier is stopped); a refusal of the Repos page's rules (a watched repository whose folder name is the same, `repoProblem`), an engine config that does not hold (`reposProblem`), a file that cannot be read or written, and an engine that kept its settings or did not answer take the line's place in orange, and nothing is written for the first.
A project with no GitHub origin has the switch off and disabled, saying why; removing a project never changes it, and the Repos page is unchanged.
The screen has an Actions row showing how many actions are set ("None" or "N actions") and a chevron, which opens an Actions screen of its own with the project's name under Actions in the toolbar.
The Actions screen has two groups, New worktree and After clone, each listing its actions in order and ending in Add action, where each action has a name, an on switch, a delete button, a monospaced command field and an "If it fails" choice of Warn and continue or Stop the session.
Each valid edit writes at once to the project's `actions` in `projects.json` (omitting `on` when on and `onFail` when Warn and continue), while a blank name or command is not written and is shown as a problem.
The screen also shows the folder with Reveal in Finder (off when the folder is not there) and the remote with Open on GitHub, only when there is one.
Its last row is Remove…, which asks "Remove <name> from Carrier?" and says that the folder, the worktrees and the sessions stay where they are and that watching is not changed (`projectRemovalWords`).
Confirming takes the entry out of `projects.json` and deletes the project's image copy in `app/project-icons` unless another project names it (`ProjectsModel.remove`, `applyingProjectRemoval`: the file is read again just before and every other entry and key is kept), then goes back to the list.
It never writes `engine.json`, so a watched project stays watched, and it touches nothing on disk but those two; the sessions keep their folder and group as a plain folder would.
A project whose folder is gone (`ProjectRow.status`, `notFound`, from `projectFolderExists`, read each time with the list) shows "Folder not found" in orange in its list row in place of its remote or path, with a dash under Disk, and its screen opens with a Folder not found row that has Locate….
Locate… opens the system's folder panel; the chosen folder goes through the same check as Add project (`projectFolderRefusal`: a file, a folder that cannot be read, one inside Carrier's own folders or a worktree of another project is refused with the reason) and a folder that is another project's is refused too, and then `ProjectsModel.locate` replaces only the entry's `path` (`applyingProjectLocation`: name, tags, icon, defaults and actions stay, and so does every key the app does not know).
The screen then opens that project at its new path as any other; the sessions that remember the old path are not rewritten and stay where they were.
The list and each project's screen follow the file while the page is open: `ProjectsModel.follow` reads it again every second, so what the chat host or an editor writes shows without reopening the page.
A file that does not match its schema, or cannot be read, shows each problem on the page with its field named (`projects.json: projects.0.name must not be blank`) in place of the list, and the page writes nothing while it is wrong.
A project's name and icon show wherever the project does, not only on its page.
A sidebar group, the rows of a session (in the sidebar, the Sessions home and the archive) and its hover card take the title and tile of the project whose folder is the session's base folder, and the rows and card of an epic take the tile of the project whose `origin` is the epic's repository (`owner/name`, case aside).
Two projects with one name keep the parent folder added to the title of their groups (`dev/Site`, `me/Site`), as two folders do.
A folder that is not a project, and a project with nothing set, draw as they always did: the folder's name and initials on a colour made from it, and an epic of a repository no project has as its `origin` draws as before.
The Repos page is unchanged.

## Changes apply without a restart

Nothing here restarts Carrier: every writer of `engine.json` or `providers.json` (the Behavior and Repos tabs, `GlobalRoleSave`, `RepoRoleSave`, `RoleDetailsSave`, `LeadReviewerSwitch`, the Engine page's GitHub identity (`saveOrchestratorProfile`), the Providers page and the new session's watch) asks the engine after its own successful write, off the main thread and off the cooperative pool (`reloadInBackground`, which hands the ask to `ReloadStatus.shared` in `CarrierCore/EngineReload.swift`; the new session and the Providers store use `ReloadStatus.answer`, which keeps the answer in the same place and gives it back for their own line).
The answer is a `ReloadOutcome` (applied, nobody to ask, an engine that does not list `reload`, refused with the engine's words, or no answer); `ReloadStatus` keeps the last one for every page, drops the answer of an older ask when a newer one was made and forgets it when Carrier stops or starts (`PanelView`), `reloadWords` says what a page shows (`ReloadNote` in Repos and Agents, with Ask Carrier again for a problem, which is said to VoiceOver every time it comes, as are the refusals of a repo's role form (`ProblemLine`); the Behavior header, which has Ask Carrier again too; the Providers lines, whose Add button waits while `ProvidersStore.busy` (Save, Remove and Add account close at once and the answer comes after), and where an older engine is a grey `editNote` and not an `editProblem`); an older engine's "reads it when it starts" is a quiet note, not a problem, and the Repos page's note follows the engine's `reload` capability and that outcome (`CarrierCore/SettingsNote.swift`, tested in `SettingsNoteTests` and `ReloadStatusTests`).
