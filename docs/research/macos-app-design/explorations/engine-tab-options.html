Engine tab: what it is for and what to do with it

What it is today

It is the setup the app needs to start Carrier (the Start Carrier button and menu item): the path of carrier, the path of node, the PATH the engine runs with, and the config file. They are found once, from your shell, when the app first opens; after that they rarely change. The readiness banner above it is shown on every tab, also when everything is fine. With Menu bar and General gone, the tab no longer has a sibling that explains it, and “Detect again”, the start log and “Reset” have lost their place too.
