# Dictation

Dictation in the message boxes: the recognizers, the microphone, the Dictation page of [Settings](settings.md) and the speech models.

## Recognizer and microphone

A real build registers Apple's recognizer (`Carrier/AppleRecognizer.swift`, over SpeechAnalyzer: native partial text, nothing to download, the language chosen on the Dictation page, which starts on the first language of macOS that it supports) and the microphone capture (`Carrier/MicrophoneCapture.swift`, which gives the buffers to the recognizer and the level, `CarrierCore/AudioLevel.swift`, about thirty times a second).
The first click asks macOS for the microphone; a denied one draws `mic.slash` and a click opens a popover with Open System Settings; a failure to start shows its cause in red under the box and is appended, cause only, to `<Carrier folder>/app/logs/dictation.log` (`DictationLog.swift`).
SpeechAnalyzer needs only the microphone permission, not `NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription`.

## Under `--fixture`

Under `--fixture` the app registers the fixture recognizer instead (`CarrierCore/FixtureRecognizer.swift`) and asks macOS for nothing: the microphone in the message box of the new session and of a conversation answers a click with a canned Spanish phrase, word by word as partial text (the normal colour with a touch of the accent and a dotted underline) and then final (plain), at the caret.
`--fixture-dictation-at-the-end` gives the phrase only after the click that stops it (the box says "Transcribing…") and `--fixture-dictation-fails` fails to start with a cause shown in red under the box.

## The Dictation page

The Dictation page (`Carrier/DictationTab.swift`, `SettingsPage.dictation`, the App group right after Chat) has the switch (`dictation.enabled`, on by default; off takes the microphone out of both boxes at once, asks macOS for nothing and stops a dictation that listens, keeping its text), the Speech model popup (`dictation.model`; an unknown one reads as Apple's), the Language popup (`dictation.language`; `languageChoices` in `CarrierCore/DictationSettings.swift` says what each model offers) and the microphone's state with Open System Settings when it is blocked, then the speech models table (`Carrier/SpeechModelsTable.swift`; the rows and what each offers are `speechModelRows` in `CarrierCore/SpeechModelsTable.swift`: a recognizer filter, a radio on the downloaded rows that chooses the model, Download with its size, a progress bar with Cancel, the cause with Retry, a trash, and the licence linking to the model's card) and Storage (the size of the files under `<Carrier folder>/app/models`, with Show in Finder).

## Speech models

`ModelStore` (`CarrierCore/ModelStore.swift`) owns the states of the models behind a downloader port: a model lives in `app/models/<recognizer>/<model>/` and counts as downloaded when a `complete` file is there, written last, so a folder without it, left by a cancel, a failure or an app that quit, is deleted (at once, or at the next launch); a failure says no connection, no space on the disk or a file that did not check; the model in use and Apple's, which the system manages, have no delete; and nothing outside the listed models can be downloaded (`SpeechModelCatalog.problems` is the rules of the list: a unique id, a licence, a size or Apple's system marker, an https card on an allowed host).
A real build lists Apple's model, four of Whisper's (below) and six of FluidAudio's (`Carrier/FluidAudioRecognizer.swift`, on Core ML, each with the licence and the card of its model): Parakeet TDT v3 and Parakeet Ultra (25 languages with detection of the language, CC-BY-4.0; both are batch models that the core's chunking runs, `CarrierCore/ChunkedPartials.swift`, tested in `ChunkedPartialsTests`: TDT v3 shows partial text, since one pass over 10 s of audio runs at 0.015 of real time, under the rule's 0.05, by transcribing the audio of the open segment again every second of audio, a pause of 150 ms after 300 ms of speech settling the segment's text and dropping its audio, and a segment of 12 s with no pause settling by agreement the words two passes in a row start with minus the last two; the stop asks for one last pass over what is not settled; Ultra is not measured and gives its text when the person stops, so the box says Transcribing…), Parakeet EOU 120M and Nemotron Streaming EN (English only, NVIDIA Open Model License), Nemotron 3.5 Multilingual (English, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, German, Chinese and Japanese, with detection of the language, OpenMDW-1.1) and Parakeet Unified 0.6B (English only, NVIDIA Open Model License), the last four giving partial text natively as the audio arrives, at chunks of about a second.
FluidAudio's `Models.md` also lists models that are not here (Parakeet Redux, Phonon, Bark, Cohere Transcribe, SenseVoice, LFM3-Audio, Paraformer, TDT Japanese, Parakeet TDT v2, Nemotron Unified and others); the list grows by an app update.
The downloads are FluidAudio's own (`FluidAudioDownloader`, the only place FluidAudio goes online, on the person's click, with its offline flag held off while any download runs), into `app/models/fluidaudio/<model>/<repository>/`; each model loads from there with its own `loadLocal` or `loadModels(from:)`, which have no downloader, and with FluidAudio's offline mode on, so a damaged folder fails to start with its cause and never downloads again.
Whisper's four models (`Carrier/WhisperKitRecognizer.swift`: Whisper Tiny, Base, Small and the quantized Large v3 Turbo of the `argmaxinc/whisperkit-coreml` repository, MIT, 99 languages with detection of the language) go through WhisperKit's plain transcription and the core's chunking, not WhisperKit's streaming transcriber, and by the rule of `docs/research/dictation-chunked-partials.md` none of them gets partial text by chunks, so the table says When you stop and the box says Transcribing…: a model must be fast (a real-time factor of 0.05 or less for one pass over 10 s) and steady (10% or less of the words shown rewritten far back), and Tiny is fast but not steady (14.8%), Small and Large v3 Turbo are not fast, and Base was not measured; a change of one catalog field to `.chunked` turns the by-chunks layer on for a model.
`WhisperKitDownloader` (WhisperKit's `download` and then the model's tokenizer, which the library would otherwise fetch from the Hub at its first load) writes into `app/models/whisper/<model>/models/<repository>/`; the load is given the folder and told not to download, and refuses with its cause a folder whose tokenizer is gone, since the library would fetch another one.
Apple's is the only model with no downloader (`RealDownloads`); under `--fixture` the fixture recognizer lists four invented entries more and `FixtureModelDownloader` shows progress, writes a thousandth of each model's size into a temporary folder and fails one entry with no connection.
With the switch off the rows under it and the table are drawn at 40% and take no clicks (`dictationRowIsLive`; Storage and Reset stay live), and Reset (`AppPreferences.reset(.dictation)`) puts the page back to on, Apple's and the language of macOS.
